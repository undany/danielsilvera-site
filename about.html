About Daniel

I am a Psychology student, certified Mindfulness Teacher, ATP-certified Nature Guide, and Operations Consultant based in Boquete, Panama.

My work explores how attention, awareness, and living systems shape human experience and performance.

Through guiding, consulting, writing, and field observation, I investigate practical ways of cultivating clarity, resilience, and meaningful connection with the natural and organizational environments we inhabit.

This website serves as a home for that exploration through nature-based mindfulness experiences, consulting work, and writing.
